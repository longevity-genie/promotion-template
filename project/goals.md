# Goals

## What every post should drive

Every post exists to move people toward one or more of these outcomes, in priority order:

1. **Play** - make people try the character generator. Lead with the interactive experience: 100 credits, real genes, you cannot afford everything. The evidence makes the game credible, but the game is what makes the post interesting. A post that informs without making anyone click "play" has failed.
2. **Share builds** - make people share what they built. The character they created, the sculpture it generated, the choices they made and why. Social proof from real builds is the best organic reach.
3. **Contribute** - make scientists and developers contact us. Gene nominations, evidence corrections, GitHub issues from strangers, new organisms. Show the gaps and reversals that make an expert think "I can improve this."
4. **Connect** - make people suggest ideas and introduce us to experts. Geneticists, longevity researchers, synbio people, anyone working on the genes in the database.
5. **Fund** - make people suggest bioart residencies, grants, exhibition calls, and other funding sources. The project premiered at an art hackathon and the sculpture output makes it eligible for art-science funding most biotech projects cannot access.

## Project-level success metrics

| Goal | Metric |
|---|---|
| Play | Sessions and completed characters |
| Share builds | User-generated posts showing their character or sculpture |
| Contribute | Gene nominations, evidence corrections, GitHub issues from strangers |
| Connect | Introductions to experts, new collaborator conversations |
| Fund | Residency acceptances, grant applications, exhibition invitations |
| Press | Newsletter mentions, articles, podcast bookings |

## What does not serve these goals

Do not write posts that present the project as a professional knowledgebase or a scientific resource to browse. That framing serves none of the five goals. The evidence and data make the game credible - they are not the product.
