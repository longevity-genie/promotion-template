# Goals

## What every post should drive

Every post exists to move people toward one or more of these outcomes, in priority order:

1. **Play** - make an adult open https://sugar-sugar.study, consent, and complete at least 6 prediction rounds. The game is the instrument. A post that explains glucose forecasting without getting anyone to draw a line has failed.
2. **Share a result** - make people share their `/share/<id>` card. A real score on a real trace is the organic reach. Do not invent scores for the team to post; play or use the staging share command.
3. **Recruit** - move the study toward about 200 adults (about 100 with diabetes, about 100 without), including CGM users and people who have never worn a sensor. Ask rooms to send people to the study, not to admire the repo.
4. **Contribute** - bug reports, CGM file-format support, localisation. Study-design changes (new questionnaire fields, changes to the prediction task) must be discussed with the team first so the dataset stays consistent.
5. **Connect** - introductions to diabetes organisations, CGM communities, and people who already benchmark glucose models (GlucoBench and similar). The protocol is the reason a serious group would care.

## Project-level success metrics

| Goal | Metric |
|---|---|
| Play | Completed runs with at least 6 rounds (the app's useful-ranking floor) |
| Share a result | Public `/share/<id>` cards and outbound social posts that use them |
| Recruit | Adults in the study file toward the protocol target of about 200 |
| Contribute | GitHub issues and PRs that do not change the live protocol without discussion |
| Connect | Warm introductions to diabetes / CGM orgs and glucose-ML groups |

Do not publish a live participant count in a post unless you have just read it from the running site or the study CSVs. A guessed N is a standing-facts violation.

## What does not serve these goals

Do not write posts that present Sugar-Sugar as a professional knowledgebase, a diabetes-education brochure, or a dosing app. Those framings serve none of the five goals and two of them are false. The ethics clearance and the missing human baseline make the game credible - they are not the product. The product is: draw the next hour, leave a score researchers can compare to a model.
