I want to share the thing that came out of our “The New Human” hackathon. If you make a build, I would love to see the form it produces.

I worked on it with Livia and Newton. You spend 100 credits on real enhancement genes, then properties of the selected proteins drive a printable crystal. The form is generated from the data rather than drawn by hand, and the same choices produce a personal report.

The library covers 136 genes from 72 source species, with 1,134 experiments behind the entries. I like that the uncertainty stays visible: the database records where a claim stops instead of presenting every organism-level trait as ready for humans.

If you know an artist, designer, or educator who would play with it, please pass it on.
