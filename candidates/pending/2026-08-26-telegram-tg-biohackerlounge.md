I want to share a project I have been working on with Livia and Newton. If you make one build, tell me which upgrade you leave out.

I was interested in what happens when the choices are made from real biology rather than a list of superpowers. The site lets you choose 106 playable genes borrowed from 72 source species - radiation resistance from a tardigrade, regeneration from an axolotl, cancer-resistance mechanisms from a naked mole-rat.

You get 100 credits, so the budget rules out an everything-build. Behind it are 136 catalogued genes and 1,134 experiments. Some claims reach human trials or the market; others stop in a cell line or a mouse. I wanted that difference to stay visible before anyone calls something an enhancement.

If you know someone who would enjoy making a build and arguing with the ratings, send it their way.
