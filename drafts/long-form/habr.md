## Timing

Habr performs best on weekday mornings Moscow time (09:00-12:00 MSK). Avoid weekends and Friday evenings. **Flexible** - losing 30-40% reach on off-peak hours, not catastrophic.

## Prerequisites

Get a full account first by asking a contact to gift an invite from your profile, which skips the sandbox entirely. Then publish to hubs `popular_science` and `biotech`, with `3d_graphics` or `opendata` as secondary.

**This must not be a launch post.** Habr explicitly classifies direct advertising of your own project as a bad publication, and the sandbox auto-rejects press releases. Write one of these three instead, with the link appearing once, near the end, as a demonstration.

If it performs, `@habr_com` (131,385 subscribers) amplifies it for free. Afterwards, self-repost to vc.ru. Then publish an English version on `habr.com/en`.
