# Facebook group meme posts

> Standing facts and voice rules: see [PROJECT.md](../../project/PROJECT.md).
> Platform rules: see `registry/platform_rules.csv` (facebook row).
> Read the pinned rules of each group before posting.

## Format

Facebook groups respond well to meme image + short caption. Keep captions to 2-4 sentences. The meme image carries the hook; the caption adds the science and the link.

## Timing

Post one gene meme per week across different groups. Never post the same meme in two groups that share significant membership overlap. Check `registry/facebook-groups-catalogue.csv` for group categories and pick groups by topic fit.
