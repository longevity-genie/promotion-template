# Drafts

Pre-written, platform-specific posts and outreach, organised by channel. Each file drafts one or more posts for a single platform or outreach type, referencing `project/PROJECT.md` and `project/copy-pack.md` for standing facts and voice.

## What belongs in a draft file

Every draft file should have:

1. **A header** linking to PROJECT.md and copy-pack.md.
2. **A timing section** summarising when to post, with a bold one-line verdict: "Post any time", "Matters moderately", or "Matters significantly".
3. **Platform-specific engagement guidance** - the first-hour actions that determine algorithmic reach.
4. **The actual post text**, ready to copy-paste.

## Universal platform knowledge

### LinkedIn

The algorithm decides a post's fate in the first 60-90 minutes. Without early engagement it gets buried.

- Immediately after posting: team members each like and/or comment within the first hour. Real comments beat emoji reactions.
- Within 2 hours: reshare from the company/showcase page with a short intro line.
- Same day: ask 3-5 trusted contacts (individually, not in a group) to like or comment. Do not ask reciprocally - LinkedIn detects engagement-pod patterns and suppresses reach.
- Post natively, link in the body. Best reach: Tuesday-Thursday, mid-afternoon local time.

### Facebook

Facebook penalises posts with outbound links (suppresses reach ~40-60%). Two strategies:
1. Put the link in the first comment instead of the post body.
2. Use native media (image/video) with the link in the caption.

Do not stuff hashtags on Facebook - it restricts reach. Zero to two maximum. Native video gets 5-10x the reach of link posts.

### Reddit

Never solicit upvotes - vote manipulation is a sitewide bannable offence. Best times: Monday-Wednesday, 08:00-10:00 US Eastern. Each subreddit has its own rules; check them every time, as they change without notice.

### Hacker News

Write by hand - no LLM, not even for editing. Block 3 hours after posting for replies; an unanswered comment on a Show HN is the whole opportunity thrown away. If a repost is needed, email dang@ycombinator.com. Never ask anyone to upvote - HN penalises **sites** as well as accounts, so one incident can flag your domain.

### Telegram and WhatsApp (warm rooms)

Use a multi-day sequence for warm communities you are already a member of: touch 1 (the update), touch 2 (a follow-up fact or image, 2-3 days later), touch 3 (a result or thank-you, 5-7 days later). Space posts across different groups by hours, not minutes - identical text appearing in many chats simultaneously is the pattern that gets personal accounts flagged.

### Bluesky

No default algorithmic feed - hashtags drop you into curated feeds instantly, so a new account still gets reach. 300-character limit includes the tag block; count them.
