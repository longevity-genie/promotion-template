# Page post 4: The sculpture - procedural generation from biological data

## Angle

The generative design side. Lead with the physical object and the constraint that no human designs the shape. This is the post that reaches design, maker, and art-science audiences who would never click on a genomics knowledgebase.

## Attach

One of Livia's sculpture renders or (when available) a photograph of a printed sculpture. The image is what makes this post travel.

## Post

Every combination of genes in Materialized Enhancements produces a different sculpture. No one draws the shape by hand.

Molecular weight, exon count, hydropathy, disorder, isoelectric point: the biophysical properties of the genes you select become the parameters of a Voronoi shell. The geometry is procedurally generated from real biological data, not from an image model.

The hard constraint is printability. Every result across the combination space must slice and print on a consumer machine without a person checking it first. That requirement shaped the geometry more than any aesthetic preference did.

The algorithm, code and biological data behind it - 136 genes, 1,134 experiments and 72 source species - are open.
