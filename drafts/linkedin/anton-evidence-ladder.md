## Timing

**Matters moderately.** Best reach: Tuesday-Thursday, 15:00-16:00 Bucharest time (catches US East Coast morning and EU afternoon). Weekend posts lose ~40-50% reach. LinkedIn's algorithm keeps serving a post for 24-48h, so exact hour matters less than day-of-week.
