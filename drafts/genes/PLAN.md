# Gene posts - plan

Each subfolder holds per-platform drafts for one gene with a shareable angle. The pattern: a pop-culture or intuitive hook, then the surprising limitation or reversal from the database, then link to the gene page on enhancement.bio.

| Gene | Hook | Reversal / surprise | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dsup (tardigrade) | Star Trek Discovery, Harbinger Down, pop-science fame | Neurotoxic in rat cortical neurons (Escarcega 2023) | **drafted** |
| FOXO3 | Strongest human longevity association, decades of hype | Only one mouse lifespan study ever done (Inci 2025, single lab) | planned |
| HAS2 (naked mole-rat) | "Naked mole-rats don't get cancer" is a meme | Promoted tumor growth in pancreatic xenograft (Tian 2015) | planned |

## Candidates with market-available therapies

These genes already have therapies sold to adults. The angle: these are not hypothetical - they are commercial, and the evidence ladder shows exactly how far each one got.

| Gene | What is sold | Hook | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| NGF (cenegermin / OXERVATE) | FDA+EMA approved eye drops for corneal ulcers | Same molecule FDA-approved in opposite directions: add it to cornea, block it in joints (tanezumab). NNT of 3 | planned |
| APOC3 (olezarsen / plozasiran) | Two FDA-approved drugs (Ionis + Arrowhead) | You can buy a pharmacological copy of what 1 in 150 people are born with - 39% lower triglycerides, ~40% lower coronary risk | planned |
| ANGPTL3 (evinacumab / Evkeeza) | FDA-approved antibody + two Phase 1 gene-editing trials dosing patients | The "stack contradiction" - the delivery system for this editor docks on a receptor that another entry sells as a benefit to remove | planned |
| VEGF (Neovasculgen) | Russian marketing authorization since 2011; also sold at clinics in Honduras/Mexico | Split personality: peer-reviewed for saving limbs from amputation (ABI 0.33 to 0.48), but the enhancement/anti-aging version has no independent proof | planned |
| Follistatin (FST344) | Sold at ~$25k/treatment at clinics in Honduras, Bahamas, Panama, Dubai | Two evidence tracks that do not touch: Mendell's peer-reviewed muscular dystrophy trials vs. commercial claims of 11 years epigenetic age reduction (company data only) | planned |
| TERT (telomerase) | AAV-TERT sold by BioViva (300+ patients claimed) | Cancer risk is not theoretical - constitutive telomerase promoted mammary carcinomas in mice (Artandi 2002). Zero peer-reviewed validation of benefit in humans | planned |
| Klotho | Plasmid gene therapy at Prospera clinics | Overdominance: one copy of KL-VS improves cognition and lifespan, two copies make you worse. Single injection improved memory in aged macaques (Dubal), but high dose lost the benefit | planned |
| Ectoine (ectABC) | CE-marked nasal sprays, eye drops, creams (Bitop AG, Germany) | Molecule harvested from extremophile bacteria by "bacterial milking" - salt the bacteria, they make it, you collect. Simplest complete biosynthetic pathway in the library (3-gene operon). Evidence largely single-sponsor | planned |

Three tiers of regulatory status: FDA/EMA-approved (NGF, APOC3, ANGPTL3), national approval (VEGF in Russia), and commercially sold without regulatory approval (Follistatin, TERT, Klotho, Ectoine as CE device).

## Reuse chain

1. Write the LinkedIn version first (longest, most detail)
2. Adapt for Reddit (self-post, different opening, subreddit-specific framing)
3. Telegram (shorter, warm-room register)
4. Bluesky (visual hook, under 300 chars + thread)
5. Other platforms as relevant

## Cadence

One gene post per week, alternating between controversy-angle and market-therapy genes. Do not post two of the same type back to back.
