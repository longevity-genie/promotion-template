### FOXO3 - "The emperor has no mouse data"

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | "Strongest human longevity gene" - decades of supplement industry hype, media coverage, consumer genetics reports |
| **PRO** | Strongest human longevity association: replicated across multiple populations (Japanese, Hawaiian, Italian, German, Chinese, Danish), genome-wide significant. T7 evidence for association. ONE mouse Tg study (Inci 2025, Hallym U, single lab): median AND maximum lifespan both +~30% via tet-on doxycycline induction |
| **CON** | Decades of hype but only ONE mouse overexpression lifespan study from a single lab (Inci 2025). FoxO3a mediates neuron death via Bim in beta-amyloid context (Sanphui & Biswas 2013, Cell Death Dis) — the "longevity gene" is also a pro-apoptotic effector in stressed neurons. All human trials measure FOXO3 as a biomarker, none give it as therapy. ~~[RETRACTED CLAIM: "Forebrain-specific overexpression was NEUROTOXIC - killed neurons (Arruda-Carvalho 2014)" — Arruda-Carvalho 2014 papers are about fear circuits and CaMKII, not FOXO3. Citation was wrong.]~~ |
| **Sold?** | No - no activator in trials either |
| **Evidence** | T7 (association only) + T4-T5 (loss-of-function) |
| **Meme** | "Strongest longevity gene!" → zero mouse lifespan data, neurotoxic in brain |
| **Status** | planned |

### SOD2 - "The antioxidant theory's embarrassment"

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | Antioxidant theory of aging, entire supplement industry built on "free radicals cause aging" |
| **PRO** | Heterozygous knockout mice: increased oxidative DNA damage and cancer incidence (Van Remmen 2003). Removing it is clearly bad. One small study: +4% mean lifespan (Hu 2007, n=54, not replicated) |
| **CON** | 2x overexpression in C57BL/6 mice (n=97): ZERO lifespan extension despite reduced lipid peroxidation (Jang 2009). Confirmed: no lifespan extension, no change in age-related pathology (Pérez 2009). Even worse: in C. elegans, SOD-2 DELETION EXTENDED lifespan - opposite direction (Van Raamsdonk 2009). The enzyme also produces H2O2 as output, so without paired catalase you just relocate the damage. SOD mimetic trial BMX-001 for head/neck cancer: withdrawn (NCT04607642) |
| **Sold?** | No - SOD supplements don't cross cell membranes anyway |
| **Evidence** | T5 |
| **Meme** | "2x antioxidant enzyme → zero lifespan effect." Deleting it in worms makes them live LONGER |
| **Status** | planned |

### Klotho - "More is not better"

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | Named after Greek fate goddess who spins the thread of life, anti-aging hormone, 7 companies racing to sell it |
| **PRO** | ONE copy of KL-VS variant: improved cognition, associated with longevity. Protein injection improved memory in aged mice and macaques (Dubal lab, UCSF). Massive clinical interest: 97 experiments in database |
| **CON** | TWO copies of KL-VS make you WORSE than one - classic overdominance. High-dose injection in macaques LOST the benefit. Narrow therapeutic window nobody has mapped. centSIRT6-style allele frequency: twice as common in centenarians (1%) vs controls (0.5%) but study too small for significance (P=0.3). Seven companies selling without knowing the safe dose. Minicircle plasmid Phase 1 (NCT07285629), Klothea mRNA Phase 1b - both extremely early |
| **Sold?** | Yes - 7 orgs (Minicircle plasmid, Klothea mRNA, Klotho Neurosciences AAV, Avai Bio cell therapy, Jocasta protein, Klothon, Kuro-o lab) |
| **Evidence** | T7 (human association) + T5 (mouse lifespan) + T5 (primate cognition) |
| **Meme** | Two-headed goddess in a Greek kitchen: "One Klotho makes you smart. Two? Two cooks in one kitchen." |
| **Images** | `images/klotho_chat_gpt.jpg` (best), `images/klotho.jpeg` |
| **Status** | **drafted** (meme + images ready) |

### GHR (Laron syndrome) - "No cancer, no diabetes, 35 cm shorter"

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | Peto's paradox meets dating apps - Laron syndrome gives you perfect metabolic protection at the cost of 35cm height |
| **PRO** | 22-year prospective cohort (n=90 GHRD + 1,606 controls): ZERO diabetes in affected subjects, cancer absent as cause of death. Fewer arterial plaques despite elevated LDL (2024 cardiovascular follow-up) |

| **Sold?** | No |

### TP53 (elephant) - "Peto's paradox meets the replication crisis"

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | Peto's paradox - elephants don't get cancer despite having 100x more cells. They have 20 copies of p53 |
| **PRO** | Elephant cells undergo apoptosis more readily than human cells. TP53-RTG9 (retrogene) triggers apoptosis in TP53-null human cancer lines (6 independent cell lines). Large body of TP53 biology |

Multiple MDM2 antagonist clinical trials terminated or withdrawn (NCT03975387, NCT04638309, NCT06090318, NCT06369871). Commercial route (Peel Therapeutics) is protein delivery, not gene transfer |

| **Meme** | "Just add more p53!" → can't reproduce the binding, Tg mouse data not even peer-reviewed |

### Lin28a (axolotl) - "Regeneration... but only if you're a baby"

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | Axolotl regeneration is iconic - plushies, TikTok, "the animal that regrows its limbs" |
| **PRO** | Overexpression enhanced tissue repair in neonatal mice - hair regrowth, ear hole closure, digit tip regeneration. iPSC factor in human cells (T6). Real regenerative potential in young animals |

## Tier 2 - Strong contradiction, needs a paragraph not a meme

### NGF (cenegermin/Oxervate) - "FDA approves adding it AND blocking it"

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **PRO** | FDA+EMA approved eye drops for neurotrophic keratitis (Dompé). NNT of 3. T10 evidence - the highest in the entire database. Corneal nerve regrowth is real and replicated in two double-masked RCTs |
| **CON** | Same molecule blocked in joints (tanezumab) for pain relief. Same protein, opposite therapeutic direction depending on tissue. 13.6% relapse at 48 weeks (3/22 healed patients). High cost per treatment course. NGF applied to the rest of the body's TrkA neurons is completely uncharacterized |
| **Sold?** | Yes - FDA+EMA approved (Oxervate, Dompé) |
| **Evidence** | T10 |

### CETP - "Human genetics says yes, every drug says no"

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **PRO** | Protein-truncating variants raise HDL, lower LDL, reduce coronary disease odds (n=58,469). The human genetic phenotype is unambiguously protective |
| **CON** | Torcetrapib INCREASED MORTALITY in a large outcome trial. Dalcetrapib failed for futility. Anacetrapib barely moved the needle. Three drugs tried, three failures or worse. Nobody knows why being born without the protein protects you but blocking it in middle age does not. No gene therapy program exists |
| **Sold?** | No (obicetrapib in trials, different mechanism) |
| **Evidence** | T7 (genetics, Very High) / Low-Medium (translation) |

### ANGPTL3 (base editing) - "Irreversible genome editing, 6 months of primate data"

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **PRO** | Two Phase 1 gene-editing trials actively dosing patients (Verve NCT06451770, CRISPR Therapeutics NCT07491172). Human LOF genetics: OR 0.59 for coronary disease (n>58,000). FDA-approved antibody (evinacumab/Evkeeza) already on market. Primate data: editing confined to liver, <2% off-target in 17 other tissues |
| **CON** | Myocardial infarction arm did NOT reach significance (OR 0.66, P=0.09). Only 6 months primate durability. IRREVERSIBLE - no undo if recipient later needs lipid transport (pregnancy, wasting illness). Transient liver enzyme rises after dosing. Paradox: the GalNAc-LNP delivery docks on ASGR1, which another gene in this library would delete. No cardiovascular outcome trial - the "prevents heart attacks" claim is 100% from carrier genetics |
| **Sold?** | Phase 1 gene editing (Verve, CRISPR Tx) + FDA-approved antibody (Regeneron) |

## How to pick the next gene to write

1. Does it have a one-sentence reversal that surprises even biologists? (+3)
2. Is the pop-culture reference recognizable to non-scientists? (+2)
3. Does the evidence SPLIT (works here, fails there) rather than just being weak? (+3)
4. Is it commercially sold, making the evidence gap urgent rather than academic? (+2)
5. Has a specific negative experiment been published (not just "needs more data")? (+2)

Score 8+ and it belongs in Tier 1. Best next candidates after Dsup: FOXO3 (the emperor has no mouse data), SOD2 (antioxidant collapse), or Klotho (dosing paradox).
