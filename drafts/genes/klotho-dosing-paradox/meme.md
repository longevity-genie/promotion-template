## Science

- Klotho named after Greek fate goddess who spins the thread of life
- ONE copy of KL-VS variant: improved cognition, associated with longevity across multiple populations
- TWO copies of KL-VS make you WORSE than one — classic overdominance
- Protein injection improved memory in aged mice and macaques (Dubal lab, UCSF), but high-dose injection in macaques LOST the benefit
- Narrow therapeutic window nobody has mapped
- Seven companies selling (Minicircle plasmid, Klothea mRNA, Klotho Neurosciences AAV, Avai Bio cell therapy, Jocasta protein, Klothon, Kuro-o lab) without knowing the safe dose
- Minicircle plasmid Phase 1 (NCT07285629), Klothea mRNA Phase 1b — both extremely early
