# WhatsApp posts

> Standing facts and voice rules: see [PROJECT.md](../project/PROJECT.md) and [copy-pack.md](../project/copy-pack.md).

## Timing

**Post any time.** WhatsApp is a messaging app - no algorithm, no timing penalty. Messages sit in the group until people read them. Slightly better engagement during evening hours (18:00-21:00 Bucharest) when people check groups casually, but the difference is negligible. **Flexible.**

## Rules for warm groups

You have been in the room for years, so write like a member with news, not a marketer with a campaign. One paragraph, one link, one ask. Never post the same text in two groups that share members.

## Multi-day sequence strategy

Split outreach into 2-3 touches over 5-7 days, each with a different angle. WhatsApp groups scroll fast, so spacing your messages gives more chances to be seen by different people at different times.
